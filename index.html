Dear Melise,

I’m genuinely drawn to you.

I think I first started feeling this way when I came across your Instagram and saw you for the first time. I know that might sound silly, and I’m sorry if it does.

I don’t really see much of a chance for me to ever have a conversation with you, but all I ever wanted was for you to be a small part of my life, even if only for a little while. I wished I could have a moment to sit with you, face to face, and simply talk.

I know I’m probably not the man you’re looking for. I’m not your age, I’m not handsome, and I’m certainly not rich. But even a short conversation with you would genuinely mean a lot to me. It would make me happy in a way that might be difficult to explain.

Maybe what you’re reading sounds foolish, or even a little pitiful. Maybe it is. I just wanted you to know.

And I’m sorry if the way I’m saying all of this makes me seem strange. I never meant to make you uncomfortable. I only wanted to be honest about why I’m writing this to you.

I don’t expect anything from you.

I simply wanted, for once, to let my feelings speak for themselves.

With all my heart,
for you. ♥️
